# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Zakaria — single user, personal life OS. Uses the app at a desk/laptop throughout the day (tasks, watching, sports) and in batch at end of day (habits). Not mobile. The app is always open, always visible; it is a control center for his entire life.

## Product Purpose

HEGON is a personal digital second brain that centralizes all of life in one place: tasks, watching (films/series/anime), sports (football, tennis, F1), habits, goals, books, journal, and more. Success looks like: opening the app and knowing exactly where everything stands, without friction.

## Brand Personality

Refined, Fluid, Personal.

HEGON feels like a personal OS built to Apple standards — not a SaaS tool, not a generic dashboard. It is distinctly Zakaria's. Each module has its own identity and color, but the whole feels coherent. It moves like something alive.

## Anti-references

- **Vercel** — too black/white, no color personality, sterile
- **Generic SaaS dark mode** — flat zinc everywhere, same card grid repeated, no soul
- **Letterboxd design** — can reference its features, never its visual language
- **AI-generated interfaces** — cream backgrounds, hero metrics, identical card grids, eyebrow labels on every section, gradient text

## Design Principles

1. **Identity per module** — each module has its own accent color and soul. Coherence at the system level, character at the module level.
2. **Depth over flatness** — surfaces are layered, hierarchy is visible. Never a single flat background. The eye always knows where it is in the stack.
3. **Motion is intentional** — every animation has a reason. Nothing animates by default. Motion confirms, explains, or delights — never decorates.
4. **Invisible quality** — the details you cannot consciously see create what you feel. Unseen correctness compounds into something that feels right.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: minimum contrast 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text. Full keyboard navigation. Meaningful labels on all interactive elements. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Single user (Zakaria) — no color blindness considerations required, but AA contrast maintained regardless.
